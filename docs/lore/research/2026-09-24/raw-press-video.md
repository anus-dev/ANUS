# Raw: press, blogs and video (scout pass 2026-09-24)

Raw scout output, not edited. The canon is the synthesis in this folder's README.md.

Scout angle: press, blogs, newsletters, podcasts, video, aggregators and tool directories, all languages. GitHub, X/Twitter, Reddit, Hacker News and other social platforms belong to other scouts; social posts appear here only when an outlet embedded or quoted them. Method: web search (many queries in English, Chinese, Russian, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, Portuguese, German, French), direct fetches of public pages, YouTube public page data, the Trendshift page, the AINews (smol.ai) archive, OpenAlex and arXiv for papers. Check date for every item: 2026-09-24 unless marked otherwise. Quotes in other languages are translated and marked. Some material (money figures, deal values and similar) was moved to the private file of this pass by rule.
